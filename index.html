About us

One group, built for the whole journey.

StritGRAD started as a small print and content outfit working out of a shared studio. Today it operates as three connected divisions serving brands, retailers and communities.

Our story

FROM SOWETO OUTWARD

StritGRAD was born in the heart of soweto, built on the belief that township hold township hold untapped talent, energy and entrepreneurial spirit ready to power real economic change.

TOWNSHIP FIRST APPROACH

We place township communities at the centre of everything we do. By sourcing talent, delivery services and creating opportunities locally, we ensure growth starts where it matters most.

PATHWAYS FOR YOUTH

Through commercial grounding and practical skills development. Stritgrad creates real, sustainable pathways turning young people from job seekers into contributors, entrepreneurs and future leaders

What we stand for

  • Own the outcome

    We measure ourselves on what changed, not on how busy we looked doing it.

  • Make it real

    Work that exists in the world — in feeds, on shelves, in people's hands.

  • Build people up

    Every commercial project funds skills and opportunity through the Academy.

  • Keep it honest

    Clear scopes, clear pricing, and bad news delivered early.

Division 02 — Merch & print

StritGRAD Merch

In-store merchandise for sale, plus the printing services that make it possible. We source, produce, print and replenish — from single sample runs to full retail rollouts.

Request a quote ← All services

Capabilities

What we actually do.

  • Retail product sourcing

    Apparel, headwear, accessories and gifting — spec'd for margin and durability.

  • In-store merchandising

    Planograms, display units, shelf-ready packaging and replenishment cycles.

  • Screen printing

    Plastisol, water-based and specialty inks on garments and flat stock.

  • DTF & heat transfer

    Short-run, full-colour and photographic prints with no minimum headaches.

  • Embroidery

    Multi-head embroidery for caps, polos, workwear and corporate uniforms.

  • Packaging, labels & fulfilment

    Custom packaging, woven labels, hangtags, pick-and-pack and shipping.

Process

From sample to shelf.

  1. 01

    Brief & spec

    Product type, quantities, target cost and delivery date locked in.

  2. 02

    Sample

    Physical sample or digital mock approved before anything goes to production.

  3. 03

    Produce & print

    Bulk production, decoration and quality control in our studio.

  4. 04

    Deliver & restock

    Store delivery, e-commerce fulfilment and scheduled replenishment.

Division 03 — Social development

StritGRAD Academy

Our foundation. The Academy trains, mentors and backs young people who want a career or a business in the creative economy — with real industry exposure, not theory alone.

Apply or partner ← All services

Programmes

What we actually do.

  • Work Readiness skills training

    Design, photography, print production, social content and digital basics.

  • Mentorship

    Paired with working professionals across our three divisions for a full cycle.

  • Learnerships & internships

    Structured placements inside StritGRAD and with partner businesses.

  • Community workshops

    Short-format, open-access sessions run in schools and community centres.

  • Enterprise support

    Starter equipment access, pricing help and back-office support for young founders.

  • School outreach

    Career guidance and creative-economy awareness for learners before they choose a path.

Process

How a learner moves through.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Open intake with a simple, low-barrier application process.

  2. 02

    Train

    Practical, portfolio-based training on real briefs.

  3. 03

    Place

    Learnership or internship inside the group or a partner business.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Employment, freelance pipeline or a supported enterprise of their own.
